Understanding Your Carbon Footprint

Calculating your carbon footprint is the first step towards effective environmental management. It involves assessing emissions across different scopes:

Scope 1: Direct Emissions

Emissions from sources your company owns or controls directly (e.g., fuel combustion in owned vehicles, on-site furnaces).

Scope 2: Indirect Emissions - Purchased Energy

Emissions from the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling consumed by your company.

Scope 3: Indirect Emissions - Value Chain

All other indirect emissions in your value chain (e.g., purchased goods, business travel, waste disposal, employee commuting).
